
# Xngloinglish Phonetic Mapping Chart

This document outlines the complete 38-character phonetic mapping for **Xngloinglish** (composed of standard lowercase characters `a-z` and 12 uppercase modifiers `B C D G J K N Q R S T Z`). 

The system relies on a strict 1-to-1 relationship between characters and speech sounds, heavily pairing lowercase and uppercase characters into unaspirated, aspirated, flapped, and sibilant consonantal sets.

---

## 1. Core Vowel System
Because `x` and `a` have distinct, dedicated roles, the standard English vowel configurations shift as follows:

| Character | Phonetic Role / Sound | English/Hindi Example | Xngloinglish Spelling |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **x** | Short A | cat, bat, fat | kxt, bxt, fxt |
| **a** | Broad A / AH sound | car, far, father / Hindi: car | kar, far, faTr |
| **e** | Short E | met, bed, hen | met, bed, ven |
| **i** | Short I | sit, pin, kit | sit, pin, kit |
| **u** | Short U | cup, hug, cut | kup, vug, kut |
| **o** | Short O | top, fox, hot | top, fox, vot |

## 2. Consonantal Pairings & Special Rules

### Palatal & Sibilant Series
* **c**: Unaspirated CH sound ($\text{c}$) (English *church*, *chip*, Hindi *chammach*)
* **C**: Aspirated CH sound ($\text{c\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *chhatri* $\rightarrow$ **Ctri**)
* **z**: Standard voiced palatal affricate ($\text{\textsubscript{\c{z}}}$) (English *jam*, *gem*, Hindi *zindagi*)
* **Z**: Aspirated voiced palatal affricate ($\text{\textsubscript{\c{z}}\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *jhanda* $\rightarrow$ **Znda**)
* **s**: Standard S sound ($\text{s}$) (English *sun*, *rice*, Hindi *safed*)
* **S**: Post-alveolar SH sound ($\text{\textsubscript{\c{s}}}$) (English *shame*, *crash*, Hindi *shahar*)

### Velar Series
* **k**: Unaspirated K sound (English *cat* $\rightarrow$ **kxt**)
* **K**: Aspirated KH sound ($\text{\c{kh}}$) (Hindi *khrosh*, *khargosh*)
* **g**: Hard, unaspirated G sound (English *government*, *goat*)
* **G**: Hard, aspirated GH sound (English *ghost* $\rightarrow$ **Gost**, Hindi *ghora*)

### Dental Series (Voiceless & Voiced)
* **j**: Soft dental T ($\text{t̪}$) (Hindi *tarbuz* $\rightarrow$ **jarbuz**)
* **J**: Soft dental TH sound ($\text{t̪\textsuperscript{h}}$) (English *thin* $\rightarrow$ **Jin**, English *throw* $\rightarrow$ **Jro**)
* **q**: Soft dental D ($\text{d̪}$) (Hindi *dawai* $\rightarrow$ **qwi**)
* **Q**: Soft dental DH sound ($\text{d̪\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *dhaniya* $\rightarrow$ **Qniya**)

### Retroflex Series
* **t**: Unaspirated retroflex T sound ($\text{\ mansions\ t}$) (English *top*, Hindi *tamatar*)
* **T**: Aspirated retroflex TH sound ($\text{\ mansions\ t\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *thela*, *thug*)
* **d**: Standard retroflex D sound ($\text{\ mansions\ d}$) (English *dog*, Hindi *damru*)
* **D**: Aspirated retroflex DH sound ($\text{\ mansions\ d\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *dhakkan* $\rightarrow$ **Dkkn**)
* **r**: Standard alveolar R trill/tap (English *run*, *red*)
* **R**: Retroflex flap RR sound ($\text{\ mansions\ ɽ}$) (Hindi *ped* $\rightarrow$ **peR**)

### Labial Series
* **b**: Standard unaspirated B sound (English *boy*, *rob*)
* **B**: Aspirated BH sound ($\text{b\textsuperscript{h}}$) (Hindi *bhalu* $\rightarrow$ **Balu**)

### Special Semi-Vowels & Nasals
* **v**: Standard H sound (English *horse* $\rightarrow$ **vors**)
* **w**: Standard W sound (English *water* $\rightarrow$ **wotr**)
* **N**: Velar nasal NG sound (English *king*, *zinc*). **Rule:** *Must always be followed immediately by `k`, `K`, `g`, or `G`.*
